This is the lever for control of the rods. The further you raise them, the more the temperature and pressure will rise.
If there are full down, the temperature will be stable and the pressure will be falling, due to natural pressure loss.
If the temperature exceeds 2000 degrees or if the pressure exceeds 250 bar, there is a meltdown. The game will restart then.
Your progress (generated power, coins, upgrades) will be lost.


This is the lever for control of the turbines. If you raise them, the power of the turbines will raise as well.
The turbines will decrease temperature and pressure and convert them to power. The max energy production is 25MWh.
The turbines only generate power when there is temperature and pressure to decrease. When turbines generate power, their own temperature rises. When this happens, you also get coins.
If the temperature of the turbines gets too high, they will stop working.
